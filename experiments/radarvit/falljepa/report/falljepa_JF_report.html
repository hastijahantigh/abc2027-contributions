JF: a join-free fall detector on the subject-held-out CV

JF finds falls from the radar signal alone. It uses no join detector, no source recordings and no labelling rule. Fall queries propose events in every 32 s window. A learned stream stage then groups the proposals from overlapping windows and decides once per fall. It scores .752, against .788 for RS and .778 for Amir's chain, which both rely on joins. All three are scored on the same 40 held-out streams.

  • JF is the best join-free model so far, but it trails the join-based models by 0.026–0.036 pooled. The dense head with Viterbi scores .632, the stage-1 queries with a fixed merge score .684, and the full model scores .752. The learned stream stage is the largest single gain.
  • JF wins fold A clearly (.799 vs RS .698, Amir .583). On A, the join-based models miss about half of the falls of subjects 2 and 3. JF keeps its precision at 0.97 there.
  • The gap comes from fold D, the official split (.706 vs RS .852, Amir .922). JF has 41 false positives there against 13 for RS, and 40 misses against 15. On fold B it is behind RS by 0.07, mostly from false positives (22 vs 5).
  • Boundaries are as good as RS's. The median start error is 0.10 s for both. Amir's rule places starts at 0.02 s because it aligns them with the join crossfade, which JF does not use by design.
  • The raw weights are unstable, and SWA rescues them. On fold A, the raw score falls from 0.67 after epoch 5 to 0.44 after epoch 15. The SWA model scores 0.80. On fold D, the last raw epoch (0.724) beats SWA (0.706). Nothing is selected on held-out data, so the reported model is SWA.

1. The model

JF keeps the RS/AP trunk and replaces everything that reasoned over recordings. Stage 1 proposes falls inside each window. The stream stage then sees every proposal of the same fall, from all the windows that saw it. It reads the stream around the proposals and gives at most 2 falls per group. One training run writes all three decodings, so each stage's contribution comes from the same weights. The model has 663k parameters, against 524k for RS.

Training

ITEM

Chunk of 4 windows

50 s of stream, cut into 4 windows 6 s apart, exactly as at inference. Chunks lie on an 8 s grid.

4 × 1,600 steps × 3 radars
BATCH

4 chunks × 2 accumulated

Amir's balanced sampler gives 2 fall chunks, 1 Autre chunk and 1 other per batch. An optimizer step sees 32 windows.

6.4 GB peak (7 GB cap)
GROUPS

Real + helper groups

The model's own proposals (≤ 6 groups), plus each uncovered true fall jittered by 0.3 s, plus 1 random no-fall region.

ROBUST

Noisy proposals

Members are dropped with p .25 (at least one is kept) and the reference span is jittered by 0.2 s.

RECIPE

As RS

15 epochs, AdamW 1e-3, radar dropout .15, dense SWA from epoch 3. The SWA model is validated.

The stream stage helps on every fold. Its largest gain is on fold A (+0.15), where it mostly recovers falls the fixed merge loses: 97 found against 70. Recall is capped by stage 1, because a fall that no query proposes has no group. The proposal recall (logged during the run) is 0.75–0.91, and the final recall is 0.70–0.78. On fold A, most misses are falls that stage 1 never proposed. On B, C and D, the refiner also rejects 0.14–0.19 of the falls that were proposed.

4. Training stability

On A and B, the raw model gets worse as training goes on: on A, recall at tIoU .3 falls from 0.54 to 0.30. On C and D it gets better. SWA beats the last epoch on A, B and C, and is 0.018 below it on D. There is one seed per fold, and run-to-run noise is about 0.02–0.03. So the gaps on C (−0.013) and in the D epoch-vs-SWA comparison are within noise. The per-fold gaps on A (+0.10), B (−0.07) and D (−0.15) are not. The pooled gap to RS (−0.036) needs more seeds before it is settled.

5. Next steps

  1. Fold D false alarms

    Compare the slot P distributions in val-validate.probs.npz across all folds. Raise the 0.5 threshold only if a single value helps every fold, and never tune it on one fold. Break D's 41 false positives down by ADL type.

  2. Batch diversity

    Each step holds only 8 independent places, against 32 for RS. Try 2-window chunks × 8 (--data.chunk_windows 2 --data.batch_size 8) at the same memory. This may also calm the raw-weight drift.

  3. Physics ablation and seeds

    Run event_physics false to measure the event physics, and add 2 more seeds before claiming any difference below 0.05.
