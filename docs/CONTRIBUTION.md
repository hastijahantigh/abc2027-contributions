# Contribution and attribution

Hasti Jahantigh carried out the experiments assigned to her in the ABC2027 team project. Her contribution included adapting and running the experiments, testing and evaluating the models, analyzing the results, and reporting her findings to the team.

The Anchor-TCN, hierarchical Transformer and signal-postprocessing experiments form a development study. The later TCN CV experiment evaluates that pipeline under the shared subject-held-out setup. Her FallJEPA/JF6 experimental track integrates and evaluates the supplied RadarViT encoder and examines event-level fusion with the existing JF6 baseline.

The shared FallJEPA/JF6 source, RadarViT encoder supplied in the handover, fold-building tools, and official annotation/evaluation helpers are foundations of this work. Vision-TriRadar informed the signal-postprocessing adaptation. The presence of these sources does not assign their sole authorship to this repository's contributor. Existing file-level attribution is preserved; no new blanket license is imposed on shared code.

The associated team manuscript is *FallPGR: Proposal Grouping and Refinement for Temporal Fall Localization in Continuous Multi-Radar UWB Streams*, by Mehdi Mohebbi, Amir Mohammad Basuti, Bahar Marashi, Hasti Jahantigh, Mohammad Mahdi Azizi, Melika Khosravi, and Bagher BabaAli, University of Tehran. The manuscript documents the team’s final FallPGR model and ensemble. This repository documents Hasti’s assigned experiments, including the FallJEPA/JF6 runs.
